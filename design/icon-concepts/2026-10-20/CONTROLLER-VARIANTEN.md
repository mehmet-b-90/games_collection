## Verwendete Prompts

### Variante 04

Use case: logo-brand. Input image 1 is the approved style and concept reference for Games Collection, a videogame collection app. Create ONE new standalone square icon variation in this same direction. Preserve the recognizable combination of a white gamepad in front of three colorful videogame cover cards, deep midnight navy full-bleed background, violet-purple-blue palette and cyan face buttons. Keep the friendly polished vector-like illustration and subtle layered depth of the reference. No text, letters, watermark, external logos, presentation layout, or device mockup. No pre-rounded outer canvas corners. Clear central composition and generous background margins for app icon cropping. Variant A — refined cinematic cover fan. Make the white controller slightly more compact and elegant, with rounded grips, a dark navy cross-shaped d-pad and four cyan circular buttons. Behind it, a clearly separated symmetrical fan of three rounded game-cover cards: violet castle silhouette on the left, purple mountains with moon in the taller middle card, blue floating island on the right. Simplify the scenic silhouettes to a few bold shapes while keeping their visual charm. Subtle smooth gradients and delicate lavender edge shading, no glow. Arrange the whole emblem as a balanced compact group taking about 70 percent of canvas width, visually cohesive, premium and readable at small sizes. This should be a fresh design inspired by the reference rather than an exact duplicate.

### Variante 05

Use case: logo-brand. Input image 1 is the approved style and concept reference for Games Collection, a videogame collection app. Create ONE new standalone square icon variation in this same direction. Preserve the recognizable combination of a white gamepad in front of three colorful videogame cover cards, deep midnight navy full-bleed background, violet-purple-blue palette and cyan face buttons. Keep the friendly polished vector-like illustration and subtle layered depth of the reference. No text, letters, watermark, external logos, presentation layout, or device mockup. No pre-rounded outer canvas corners. Clear central composition and generous background margins for app icon cropping. Variant B — compact graphic collection badge. Develop a bolder, cleaner alternative using the same white controller and three game-cover cards. Use a tight overlapping arc of three short wide rounded cards behind the controller, so the overall emblem has a compact nearly circular silhouette. Card illustrations are very minimal and large: a simple purple castle, violet mountain peak and pale moon, blue floating island, no tiny scenery. Front controller has a strong smooth silhouette, dark navy d-pad and four bright cyan buttons. Reduce bevels and depth, prefer crisp flat color planes with only restrained gradient accents, thick confident forms and excellent negative space. Keep the motif centered within central 70 percent of the square canvas. Maintain the same inviting gaming mood as the reference, but with a visibly more minimal and bold app-icon treatment.
